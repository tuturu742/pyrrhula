## Goal
Extend F3.9's pack matrix to `swdev` and make the v1.2 INV-9 wording ("every shipped pack")
operational: boot the swdev pack, run its smoke session, assert zero core diffs — including
the genericity claim F3.13 makes about rendering: a `work_item` renders through F3.10's
existing tag→widget registry with **zero widget additions**.

- [x] Carry the documentation side of the v1.2 INV-9 rewording: plan §4.2 / agent-guide /

> **Scope note.** "Add `swdev` to the matrix" required literally zero harness code —
> `tests/packs/test_pack_matrix.py` already discovers pack directories generically
> (F3.9's own design), so F3.13 shipping `packs/swdev/` with a valid `smoke.json` was
> the entire "extension." The only matrix-file change here is tightening
> `test_pack_matrix_is_not_empty`'s guard from `>= 2` to `>= 3`, so a future regression
> back down to two packs fails loudly. The two genuinely new tests
> (`tests/packs/test_inv9_extension.py`) needed swdev-specific setup the generic smoke
> fixture doesn't cover: a real suspend/checkpoint/restore/resume cycle on the
> `plan_implement_review_merge` process, and a field-by-field widget-id check across
> all three swdev schemas (not just the one schema `smoke.json` exercises). The
> "triage → plan → review" wording in this task's own original subtask text was
> simplified to "seed directly at `implement`, suspend, resume to `review`" — mirroring
> `test_awaits.py`'s own established "fast-forward past the turn-taking machinery,
> exercise only the await itself" pattern, since walking a real `mode:"free"` actor
> through `triage`/`plan` first would exercise the scheduler/turn-taking machinery this
> task isn't about (a real actor turn on a placeholder delegation actor has no outcome
> to script yet).

## Acceptance criteria
- All three packs boot and complete smoke sessions in CI on an unmodified core.
  → `test_all_shipped_packs_boot_and_run_smoke_sessions` (matrix now includes swdev)
- The swdev smoke suspends at the `implement` `await` and resumes to `review` on an
  injected delegation outcome — surviving a checkpoint/restore across the suspension.
  → `test_swdev_smoke_suspends_at_delegation_await_and_resumes_on_injected_outcome`
- The tag→widget registry is byte-identical before and after swdev pack load (zero widget
  additions).
  → `test_swdev_pack_adds_zero_widgets_to_the_registry`

## Design decisions
- **The stub is the contract.** By asserting suspend/resume against an injected outcome now,
  G4.16 inherits a fixed interface instead of negotiating one — and the pack is proven
  generic without waiting a phase for MCP.

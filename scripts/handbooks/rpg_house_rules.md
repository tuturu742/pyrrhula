# House rules — the Ashen Vale table

## Setting
Campaigns take place in the Ashen Vale, a fog-bound valley where iron rusts
overnight and the dead pay tolls. The ferry town of Graymoor is the hub.

## Table rules
- Every check states the stakes BEFORE the roll: what success and failure mean.
- A natural minimum is never "nothing happens" -- the situation always worsens.
- Player secrets are written on cards and given to the facilitator; revealing
  another player's secret without cause costs a fate token.

## Fate tokens
Each player starts a session with two. Spend one to reroll, or to add a true
detail to the scene. The facilitator may award one for a costly, in-character
choice.

# Northlake Coffee — company profile

## Who we are
Northlake Coffee is a specialty roaster with 14 cafés across the lake district and a
subscription roast-at-home program. Founded 2014; roastery in Harborview.

## Brand voice
Warm, plainspoken, no puns. We never call customers "guests". The word "artisanal"
is banned in all copy. Tagline in current use: "Roasted close to home."

## Marketing rules
- Every campaign names ONE hero product. Current hero: the Harborview Blend.
- Budget approvals above 40,000 crowns require the finance chair's sign-off.
- Seasonal campaigns launch on the first Monday of the season, never mid-week.
- Loyalty members (the "Northlake Circle") always get 48-hour early access.

## Audience
Core: commuters 25-45 within walking distance of a café. Growth: subscription
gifting. We do not advertise on billboards; word-of-mouth and local partnerships.
